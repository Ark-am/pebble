Keep the important times around the world with World Clock. A clear digital watch-face for time zones around the world. Your local time takes centre stage beside the day and date, with the time of two other cities underneath.

Choose from 49 cities around the world and give each one your own label. Each city shows its time difference from yours and the day when it differs, making it easier to plan a call or follow up on a loved one. Half-hour and 45-minute time zones are supported as well.

Features:
- 12-hour or 24-hour time displays
- Personalize the time and text fonts
- Choose background, time, and accent colours on colour watches, or use a light or dark background on black-and-white models

Set everything up through the Pebble phone app. The paired phone refreshes time-zone offsets hourly, including daylight-saving adjustments for supported cities. When your phone is away, the watch continues using the last received offsets; reconnect to pick up any changes.
