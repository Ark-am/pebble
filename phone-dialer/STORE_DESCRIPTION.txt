Phone Dialer lets you place calls from your Pebble without reaching for your phone. Call a favourite, browse your contacts, call back someone from your recent calls, or type any number on the keypad.

Favorites shows your starred contacts, and Contacts is grouped from A to Z so you can find anyone in a few presses. Recent calls lists your latest 100 calls, with missed, incoming, and outgoing calls clearly marked. The keypad covers digits, *, #, and + for international numbers.

During a call, your watch becomes a remote. See who you're calling and their number, even for a number typed on the keypad that belongs to a contact. Watch the call go from Calling to Connected with a running timer, switch the sound between the phone, the speaker, and a Bluetooth headset or car, mute and unmute your microphone, and hang up with the Down button or by tapping End.

On Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2, tap keys, swipe through lists, and tap the call controls, or use the buttons on any Pebble. In Settings, choose a dark or light theme, arrange the main menu in the order you use most, and switch touch input and tap vibration on or off.

Android only. Phone Dialer requires its free Android companion app, which places the calls through your phone. Allow Phone access to make and end calls, Contacts for Favorites and Contacts, and Call history for Recent calls; each feature works on its own. On Android 12 and newer, link your Pebble once in the companion app to switch the sound, mute, and see the call status and timer from your watch. Your contacts and call history stay on your phone and watch and are never sent to a server. On dual-SIM phones, set a default calling SIM. For emergencies, use your phone directly.
